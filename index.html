Attend This Workshop and Learn

Six retirement topics worth
understanding before you retire.

An educational overview of the major areas to think through as you plan. Bring your questions. Leave with a clearer understanding.

01

Retirement Income Basics

An overview of how people think about turning savings into steady income in retirement, and the general categories of options that exist.

02

Taxes in Retirement

How different types of retirement accounts are generally taxed, and why the order you draw from them can matter.

03

Managing Risk in Retirement

An overview of the common risks retirees plan around — market movement, inflation, and long-term care — and the general concepts used to think about them.

04

Social Security Timing

How claiming age affects benefit amounts, and the factors people weigh when deciding when to file.

05

Medicare & Healthcare Basics

A plain-language overview of how Medicare works around age 65 and the coverage categories to be aware of.

06

Estate & Legacy Basics

An introduction to common estate-planning concepts — wills, beneficiaries, and how probate generally works.
